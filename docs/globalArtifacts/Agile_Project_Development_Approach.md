# Team B - Agile Project Development Approach

This document outlines the process our team will follow for the development of MiteLovers. Its goal is to create a shared understanding of processes, responsibilities, and expectations while maintaining alignment with the PO throughout the project lifecycle. 

**General business concept**: The MiteLovers ecosystem is centered around the trading of used books and magazines, while also promoting sustainability and the creation of a positive community around reading and authors.

At the current stage of the project, development has focused on domain modeling, implementation of core Java classes, and simple unit tests validating basic behavior.

**User stories**: User stories (US) are used to express requirements, whereas functionalities group related capabilities, and use cases describe intended system behavior. As such, user stories may require additional issues for their completion, however, they will have a primary fulfilment issue.

**Agile principles and flexibility**: US may evolve or change and introduce conflicting or overlapping requirements; issues will be created/updated accordingly.

**Distributing issues**: Each team member is assigned user stories to implement end-to-end. Issues are scheduled according to their dependencies (analysis issues precede design -> design issues precede tests & implementation). For learning purposes, each team member will develop the diagrams, code, and tests for their assigned US in their entirety.

##### Types of issues: 

- **User Story Fulfilment Issues**: at least one per US, with related sub-issues 

- **Analysis & Requirement Issues**: may result in new or updated use cases; need for clarifications of functional requirements from the PO

- **Design & Architecture Issues**: creating or updating PlantUML diagrams (OOD, SD, etc.), Pure Fabrications, etc.

- **Implementation Issues**: coding and technical tasks

- **Testing & Quality Issues**: cover TDD activities and test coverage requirement; includes unit tests, integration tests, improving test coverage

- **Quality Control Issues**: create a task force composed of members of the team to guarantee cohesion and the following of the team’s guidelines

**Definition of done**: The UML diagrams are created or updated; PIT test coverage of at least 95%; code is documented using JavaDoc; code is peer-reviewed; all related sub-issues are closed; the mandatory fulfilment issue is completed/achieved
